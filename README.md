# Custom Datapack and Resource Pack for the Omlet Minecraft Server

For Minecraft Java 26.3. Install both packs together: the datapack adds the features and the resource pack adds their sounds, textures and models.

---
## Datapack Functionality
- Day Counter Broadcast
    - Every few in-game days, the chat receives a message showing how many days have passed.

- Custom music discs!
    - Craft a jukebox, red dye and an iron pickaxe together for the "National Anthem of USSR" disc

- Vine Boom Goat Horn
    - Surround a goat horn with 8 TNT to get a horn that plays the vine boom

- Custom Paintings!
    - Featuring all sorts of art used for roleplay
    - They show up randomly when you place a normal painting
    - And more!

- Letter Banner Patterns
    - Every letter from A to Z is available in the loom, no pattern item needed

- Torch Arrows
    - Craft an arrow with a torch. When the arrow lands, it places a torch where it hit

- Industrial Light
    - Craft with 7 black concrete around a glowstone, with redstone in the bottom middle
    - Place it on a floor, wall or ceiling like an item frame. It gives off full light and flickers now and then
    - Sneak and right-click it to switch it on or off. Punch it to pick it back up

- Player Saddle
    - Craft with a saddle on top, a leather chestplate in the middle, an end rod underneath and leather down both sides
    - Wear it in your chest slot, and another player can right-click your back to ride on your shoulders
    - Sneak to get off

- Trash Can Multiblock
    - Build a trash can to destroy any items put into it
    - Place a glow item frame holding a lava bucket on top of a barrel, with a redstone lamp beneath the barrel
    - The trash can only burns items while the redstone lamp is powered

- Flatworld Portal
    - Build an upright portal frame out of coarse dirt, 5 blocks wide along the bottom with a 3×3 opening
    - Place a glow item frame on the middle block of the bottom, and put a diamond block in it to activate the portal
    - Stand in the portal for a moment to travel between the overworld and the flatworld
    - If there is no portal on the other side, one is built for you at ground level
    - Going back through takes you to the portal you came from
    - Breaking the frame, or taking the diamond block out, turns the portal off
